### 🧬 About Me

I am a **Bioinformatics Master's student** at the **Alma Mater Studiorum – University of Bologna** (Department of Pharmacy and Biotechnologies), bridging wet-lab biological intuition with dry-lab computational modeling.

Previously, I earned my **B.Sc. in Biology** at the **University of Siena**, where I conducted an experimental thesis in **applied functional proteomics** focused on early biomarker identification. Today, my work is fully dedicated to computational approaches for understanding macromolecular mechanisms and accelerating therapeutic discovery.

- 🔬 **Core Research Interests:** Machine Learning & Deep Learning applied to **Structural Biology**, **Protein–Protein & Protein–Ligand Docking**, and **Computational Drug Design**.
- 💻 **What I Work With:** Sequence & structural alignment pipelines, profile Hidden Markov Models (pHMMs), predictive modeling on biological and chemical descriptors, and macromolecular structure analysis.
- 🎯 **Goal:** Developing robust, interpretable dry-lab workflows that translate complex structural and omics data into actionable insights for drug discovery.
